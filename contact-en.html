What to Bring


No extensive preparation is required. Bringing a real situation, decision, or cognitive difficulty is sufficient.


A Quick Clarification session is:

  • Interactive and structured in real time
  • Focused on organising elements of a specific situation
  • Identifying relevant variables
  • Reducing cognitive noise
  • Producing a clear structural representation of the problem

Deliverables are typically provided in written structured format, sometimes supported by simplified diagrams.
